About Suman Jampany

Your trusted Bay Area real estate partner — results-driven, client-first.

Licensed REALTOR® · DRE #00000000 · Bay Area, CA

Hi, I'm Suman Jampany

With over a decade of experience in Bay Area real estate, I've helped more than 200 families buy, sell, and invest in properties across Saratoga, Los Gatos, Monte Sereno, San Jose, and Campbell.

My approach is simple: understand what matters most to you, apply deep market expertise, and fight hard for your best outcome. Whether you're a first-time buyer navigating your first offer, a move-up seller looking to maximize your proceeds, or an investor building a portfolio — I bring the same energy and commitment to every transaction.

Before real estate, I spent eight years in tech, which gives me a unique analytical edge. I build custom market models, run detailed comparable analyses, and use data — not gut feeling — to price and negotiate deals.

I'm fluent in English, Telugu, and Hindi, and I'm proud to serve the vibrant South Asian community across the Bay Area.
