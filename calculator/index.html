How It Works

Understanding Your Numbers

📊

Debt-to-Income (DTI)

Lenders look at your monthly debts vs. gross income. Most prefer back-end DTI under 43%. Lower is better — it means more buying power.

💵

Down Payment Impact

Putting 20%+ avoids PMI (typically $100–$200/mo). Less than 20% is fine — FHA allows 3.5% down with good credit.

🏦

Tennessee Taxes

TN has no state income tax on wages, making it one of the most affordable states. Property tax varies significantly by county.

📈

Rate Sensitivity

A 1% change in interest rate affects your buying power by ~10%. Use the slider to see how rate changes impact your max price.
